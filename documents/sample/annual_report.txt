GlobalCorp Annual Financial Summary 2024

Executive Summary:
GlobalCorp delivered strong top-line performance in Fiscal Year 2024. Total gross revenue across all regional operating hubs reached $9,350,000, representing a 14% year-over-year increase compared to FY 2023.

Regional Breakdown:
- North Region: Generated $3,200,000 driven by strong demand in enterprise server systems.
- South Region: Generated $2,400,000 supported by consumer product expansion.
- East Region: Generated $2,100,000 with steady growth in cloud consulting services.
- West Region: Generated $1,650,000.

Risk Factors & Data Discrepancies:
Operational audit identified currency conversion discrepancies in European hubs during Q2 due to unhedged EUR/USD fluctuations.
